ℹ️ How Our Sessions Work

  • Games are 1:1. Every game is a head-to-head match.
  • We play in rounds. A round is a “wave” where games start together.
  • Pairings are dynamic. Pairings for the next round are based on results.
  • No fixed number of rounds. Rounds continue as long as people are playing.
  • Odd number of players? You can play two separate games that round.
