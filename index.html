How It Works

  • Games are 1:1. Each game is a head-to-head match.
  • We play in rounds. A round is a wave where games start together.
  • Pairings are dynamic. Pairings for later rounds can be based on earlier results.
  • No fixed number of rounds. Rounds continue as long as people are still playing.
  • Odd number of players: someone may play two separate games against two opponents during a round/session.
